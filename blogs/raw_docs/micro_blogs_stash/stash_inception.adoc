# Microblog : Building Stash Pt1 : Inception and Planning

## The Idea

What is STASH and Why I think it would be fun to build ?

Well the reason is simple, almost all of us earning the hard buck would want to know how we are spending it ?
It's not always about saving, it can also be about optimized spending.

Some years ago we always were looking for apps that can manage our spends (can't even recall their names) they were perhaps perfect answer to the manual world.
But a perfect manual world does not exist.
Either you would have to be highly disciplined to log entry manually for all the spends that you have made in a day and continue it month over month just to share at your dashboards and eventually give up the marathon or like me, you would just give up in a couple of months.

But then the era changed.
With every delivery , purchase of inventory, retail or grocery we started getting to the digital world where our mailboxes became our digital ledgers.
But you need to find someone to create the fancy dashboard for you.
But then spend tracking is not just about reviewing fancy dashboard at the end of the month.
Perhaps with enough data and purchase history I can optimize my spending or even optimize how I am paying for them so that I can make the most out of my credit cards (but I wander too far)

Thus came the plan to create STASH (Smart Tracking Analysis and Spend Hub).
I dont think I have thought this through to be a product to be launched.
Perhaps I would be building this for myself first and take it for a ride.

## What is STASH all about ?

Idea currently is all simple in my head.
I would want to collate most of my spends.
This does not just extend to groceries, but also analyzing my card spends, UPI transactions so on and so forth.

What I plan to do with that , I might not be able to tell you about this just yet , this is still an evolving thought.

But one thing I have sorted is I have just one email where I get all my digital ledgers, and like most of us (atleast the normal one's) the mail is provided by GMAIL, which gives me rich access to my mail account (read-only).

## How do I plan to build it ?

The starting phase is going to be pretty simple (well not that simple either).
I am going to make a DIY app , host it for free (on my internal LAN system) and use anything everything open source.
This also includes free access to LLMs that I can get my hands on.

At a very high level my system architecture would look something like this

[plantuml]
----
@startuml Basic Sample
!include https://raw.githubusercontent.com/plantuml-stdlib/C4-PlantUML/master/C4_Container.puml


System_Boundary(c1, "STASH") {
    Container(web_app, "Web Application", "User interface (a REACT based app)")
    Container(service, "Analytical Engine", "serves user analytics - the core")
    Container(ume, 'User Management Engine' , "Manages user login , auth and access token management")
    Rel(web_app, ume, 'login_services')

    ContainerDb(db,'Database','Transactional multipurpose database')
    Rel_D(ume, db, 'user related detail CRUSOps')

    BiRel_D(service, db, 'CRUD ops')
    Rel_U(service, web_app, 'data')

    Boundary(bke,'Backend Engines'){

        Container(mscan, 'Scanning engine', 'Scans and downloads mails from various sources')
        Container(pen, 'Parsing engine', 'Parses , pre-processes & structurizes content for storage and post processing')
        Rel_U(mscan, ume, 'get access tokens')
        Rel_R(mscan, pen , 'scanned content')

    }

    Lay_D(db,bke)

}

System_Ext(gmail,'GMAIL','Version 1 mail / notification sources')
System_Ext(ollm,'Hosted LLMs','Public free hosted LLM points')

Rel(mscan, gmail, 'scan mails restrictied to sources')
Rel(pen, ollm, 'parse messages')
Rel_U(pen,db,'store content')

@enduml
----
